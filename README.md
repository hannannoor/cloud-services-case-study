# Cloud Services Case Study

A group research report evaluating cloud service models and infrastructure modernisation for the logistics company in the NECT case study.

**Subject:** Network and Cloud Technology (CCF1C04)  
**Semester:** Year 1, Semester 2 — AY2025/2026 October  
**Subject result:** Distinction (the overall subject grade, not an individual assessment mark)

## Read the report

- [Group case study report](docs/cloud-services-group-report.docx)
- [Assessment brief — PDF](docs/assessment-brief.pdf)
- [Assessment brief — Word](docs/assessment-brief.docx)

The report compares IaaS, CaaS, PaaS, FaaS and SaaS, evaluates the applicability of IaaS to the company's on-premises systems, and discusses operations, vendor lock-in, performance, security, cost and scalability.

## My contribution

Hannan Noor's named research section focuses on **operations and vendor lock-in risk**, including service-model comparisons, applicability to the company's existing setup, advantages, limitations and justification. The report includes references and records of AI collaboration.
